Research Project

Assessing Traceability RegTech Applications in Honey Bee and Cherry Industries and Related Supply Chains

University of Tasmania (2023 to 2027)
Funded by the Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry, Australian Government.

About the Project

This project investigates how regulatory technology (RegTech) and digital traceability solutions can support transparency, compliance, and information sharing in the Australian honey bee and cherry industries. It examines traceability requirements and technologies across producers, processors, regulators, and other supply-chain participants. The project aims to identify practical approaches for improving traceability, regulatory assurance, and supply-chain resilience.
